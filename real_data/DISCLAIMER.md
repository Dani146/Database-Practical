## Data Disclaimer

The `department.csv`, `ai_usage.csv`, and `employee.csv` files contain **fictional data** created specifically for this project.

Unlike the `Company`, `Role`, `Job_offer`, and `AI_tool` tables, which are populated using publicly available real-world datasets, these three tables use artificially generated records.

The main reason for this decision is the limited availability of publicly accessible, openly licensed data in these areas.

**AI Usage:** Artificial intelligence adoption within companies is a relatively recent and rapidly developing topic. Detailed information about which AI tools are used by specific departments, when they were implemented, and how they are integrated into business operations is generally not publicly disclosed. Companies may consider this information commercially sensitive.

**Employee Data:** Companies typically do not publish detailed information about individual employees, their positions, employment histories, or employment termination reasons due to privacy, data protection regulations, and confidentiality concerns.

**Department Data:** Although general organizational structures may be publicly available, detailed department-level information linked to individual companies, employees, and AI adoption is often incomplete or inaccessible.

Therefore, synthetic data was created to populate these tables and enable testing of the database schema, relationships, constraints, and SQL queries.

**Important:** These records do not represent actual employees, departments, or verified AI implementations within the companies included in this database.
